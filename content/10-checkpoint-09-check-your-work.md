# Checkpoint 09 — Check your work

> **Wrap up**

**When you finish this:** confidence that every piece actually works.

Run through these five yourself before you close the tab:

1. The verify script below runs clean.
2. Your `anchor test` run shows the test passing.
3. Your transfer signature from checkpoint 5 opens in the explorer.
4. Your devnet program ID opens in the explorer, with `?cluster=devnet`.
5. You can say in a couple of sentences what broke and how you fixed it. If nothing broke, say why the PDA in checkpoint 7 had no account behind it.

> **Point five is the one that matters**
> The install is worth nothing on its own — a script did most of it. What sticks is being able to describe a failure precisely enough for another person to act on it. Write yours down somewhere while it is fresh.
